Item Type: Hot Area (HA)

Purpose:
Assess ability to select correct regions within an image or screenshot.

Rules:
- Minimum 4 hot areas; maximum 8.
- Learner must select at least 2 areas.
- Do NOT use to test “where to click” alone.
- Each correct selection earns one point.

Output Requirements:
- Identify hot areas and required selections.
- Return JSON that conforms to the HA schema.
